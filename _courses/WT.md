description: "Welding Technology is a 2-year program learning proficiency in
  four different welding procedures. During the first year we teach SMAW and
  GMAW, (solid wire and flux core) while working in the lab. We also teach proper
  use of various hand tools, oxy/acetylene cutting tools, plasma cutters, mag
  drill, hydraulic press, and heavy duty shear cutter. The second year we teach
  TIG welding to complete the processes mentioned above.\nAlong with the lab
  work we are also teaching out of a textbook and workbook. In addition students
  will complete an OSHA 10 hour course as well as state mandated WIN testing.\
  \ \nDuring their second year in the classroom we will concentrate on blue print
  reading and drawing interpretation. This class is designed to prepare students
  for the work force after graduation. They are able to earn dual credits through
  IVY Tech with no additional assignments. Students will be eligible for an internship
  if all classroom and lab requirements are met. Many times an internship turns
  into a summer job and possibly a full time career.\n/Acceptance to the class
  is required so you need to apply early if possible. There is always a long
  waiting list for ones that decide to take a different path."

title: Cosmetology and Barbering
description: The cosmetology program teaches the students how to cut hair, 
  color hair, customize conditioning treatments, manicuring, pedicuring, 
  makeup, facial waxing, facials, braiding, hair pressing and hairstyling. 
  They learn about skin, hair and nail diseases and disorders and how to 
  recognize them.  They will learn in depth about infection control, 
  sanitizing and disinfection procedures to help protect themselves and 
  their guests from the spread of infection. They will be taught 
  communication skills and the ins and outs of salon business.  We talk in 
  depth about the different aspect of the salon world and different types 
  of employment opportunities, self employment, commission and hourly 
  stylist positions as well as how to do taxes and save for their future.
short_description: Cosmetology is the art and science of beautifying the 
  hair, skin and nails.  In the cosmetology program they will learn how to 
  cut, color, style and add texture to hair as well as facials, makeup, 
  waxing and nail services.  This is a two year program that will give them
  hours, progress and grades that will transfer on to the cosmetology 
  school or apprentice program they decide to attend to get their license. 
  This will give them a head start to their career, as well as reducing or 
  eliminating college debt.

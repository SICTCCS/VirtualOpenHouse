Cosmetology and Barbering

No images for Cosmetology and Barbering yet!

The cosmetology program teaches the students how to cut hair, color hair, customize conditioning treatments, manicuring, pedicuring, makeup, facial waxing, facials, braiding, hair pressing and hairstyling. They learn about skin, hair and nail diseases and disorders and how to recognize them. They will learn in depth about infection control, sanitizing and disinfection procedures to help protect themselves and their guests from the spread of infection. They will be taught communication skills and the ins and outs of salon business. We talk in depth about the different aspect of the salon world and different types of employment opportunities, self employment, commission and hourly stylist positions as well as how to do taxes and save for their future.

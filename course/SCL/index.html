Supply Chain & Logistics

No images for Supply Chain & Logistics yet!

LINK is a unique program in partnership with Amcor open to all EVSC juniors and seniors interested in exploring careers in manufacturing and logistics. Dual credit opportunities are offered through Ivy Tech Community College. While on site, students will have a first hand experience into all departments including maintenance, quality, human resources, press tech, safety, printing operations, and many more. Students will learn in a state-of-the-art, on-site classroom, gaining hands-on experience, completing labs, and developing valuable workplace skills all while earning their high school diploma. After completion of the program, students may be offered a full time position to begin their career with Amcor.
